Private by design

Your photos stay on your device

Instant Cleanup runs the LaMa inpainting model in your browser with ONNX Runtime Web. The photo you choose is processed locally instead of being sent to an image-editing server.

Simple workflow

Brush over what you want removed

Upload a photo, paint a mask over the unwanted area, run cleanup, and download the repaired PNG. You can reuse the result and clean another area without leaving the page.

Local AI

WebGPU first, WASM fallback

Supported browsers use WebGPU for local inference. When WebGPU is unavailable, the app can fall back to WebAssembly so the cleanup workflow remains browser-based.

How to remove unwanted objects from a photo

  1. Choose a photo. The image opens locally in your browser.
  2. Paint the unwanted area. Use the brush to cover the object, person, text, or distraction you want to remove.
  3. Run AI cleanup. Instant Cleanup repairs the selected region with the local LaMa model.
  4. Download the result. Save the cleaned image as a PNG or use it as the base for another edit.

What can you remove?

Use Instant Cleanup for small unwanted objects, background distractions, photobombers, signs, text, clutter, and other areas that can be reconstructed from the surrounding image. Results depend on the photo and mask, so smaller, well-defined selections often work best.

Why use a browser-based AI object remover?

Many online photo cleanup tools require uploading images before processing them. Instant Cleanup is designed for a different workflow: the model executes locally, which keeps the original photo on your device and avoids creating a server-side copy of your image. The AI model itself may be downloaded and cached by your browser when you choose a photo.

Frequently asked questions

Is Instant Cleanup free?

Yes. The browser-based object remover is free to use and does not require an account.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Image loading, masking, inference, and result generation happen in your browser.

What is AI inpainting?

Inpainting reconstructs a selected part of an image using the visual context around it. Instant Cleanup uses LaMa for this local repair step.

Why does the AI model need to load?

Local processing requires the model to be available in your browser. Instant Cleanup starts loading it after you choose an image instead of downloading it as soon as the landing page opens.
